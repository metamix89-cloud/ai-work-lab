# Yummy Tummy Foods Employee Handbook (fictional, for training)

## 1. Grade table
Associates, Specialists and Senior Associates are grade B. Managers and Senior Managers are grade A. Directors and above are grade S. Travel, hotel and corporate card limits are set by grade. Contract staff follow grade B.

## 2. Business travel hotel limit
Hotels are reimbursed at actual cost up to, per night: grade S $120, grade A $90, grade B $70. New York, San Francisco and Honolulu are expensive, so add $20 per night to each grade's limit there. Anything above the limit is paid by the employee.

## 3. Travel per diem
The travel per diem is $20 per day regardless of grade, counting both the departure and return days. A same-day trip gets $10. The per diem is paid without receipts.

## 4. Travel meals
Travel meals are reimbursed up to $10 per meal, only for meals with a receipt. Team dinners are not travel meals.

## 5. Travel transportation
Train, bus and air fares are reimbursed at actual cost. Flights are allowed only for trips of 300 miles or more one way, economy class only. Personal cars get $0.60 per mile plus tolls.

## 6. Travel requests and approval
Request a business trip with groupware form TR-101 at least 3 days before departure. International trips use form TR-110, at least 14 days before departure, with division head approval.

## 7. Travel expense deadline
Submit travel expenses with form TR-120 within 7 days of returning. Late claims move to the next month's payroll.

## 8. Corporate card limits
The monthly corporate card limit is grade S $3,000, grade A $1,500, grade B $500. Any single purchase over $300 needs the team lead's approval in advance.

## 9. Corporate card — prohibited items
The corporate card may not be used for gift cards or cash equivalents, personal items, or adult entertainment venues. Violations are fully recovered and referred to the HR committee.

## 10. Paid time off accrual
Employees with less than one year of service earn 1 day of PTO for each full month worked. After one year, employees get 15 days, plus 1 more day every 2 years from the third year, up to 25 days.

## 11. PTO requests
Request PTO at least 3 days in advance with groupware form HR-201. Half days are morning (9 AM-1 PM) or afternoon (2-6 PM). Same-day requests need the team lead's verbal approval, with HR-201 filed by the next day.

## 12. Life-event leave
Paid life-event leave: your own wedding 5 days, your child's wedding 1 day, spouse giving birth 10 days, death of a parent or parent-in-law 5 days, death of a grandparent 3 days. The request form is HR-204.

## 13. Life-event support payment
The company pays $500 for your own wedding, $500 for the death of a parent or parent-in-law, and $200 for the death of a grandparent. Apply with HR-204 and proof within 30 days.

## 14. Remote work
Remote work is allowed up to 1 day a week, with the team lead's approval by 6 PM the day before. Production and warehouse floor staff are not eligible. While remote, keep your chat status set to 'working'.

## 15. Flexible hours
Choose a start time of 8, 9 or 10 AM each quarter. Core hours are 10 AM to 4 PM. Change it with form HR-230 at least 7 days before the quarter starts.

## 16. Security — passwords
Company system passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days. You may not reuse any of your last 5 passwords. Never send passwords by chat or email.

## 17. Security — external storage
Only USB drives and other external storage registered with the security team may be used. Register a device with form SEC-07; registered devices are re-checked every year. Using an unregistered device gets a first warning, then an HR committee referral.

## 18. Security — generative AI
Do not enter customer personal data, supplier prices or unreleased product information into generative AI tools (ChatGPT and the like). On the company-approved account (the internal AI portal), summarizing internal documents is allowed. Violations are handled as security violations.

## 19. Security incident reports
Report personal data leaks, clicks on malicious email and lost laptops to the security team at extension 7119 immediately, and file an incident report with form SEC-12 within 24 hours.

## 20. Purchasing — quotes
Purchases of $1,000 or more need at least 2 quotes; $10,000 or more need at least 3. Repeat purchases from the same vendor within a year move to an annual price contract.

## 21. Purchasing — approval
Purchases under $5,000 are approved by the team lead, $5,000 up to $50,000 by the division head, and $50,000 or more by the CEO. The form is PU-301.

## 22. Vendor registration
Register a new vendor with form PU-310, with a tax ID form, a bank letter, and a food safety certificate (for food ingredient vendors only). No orders before registration.

## 23. Customer return rules
Customer returns: frozen 7 days, refrigerated 3 days, pantry 14 days (delivery day included). Opened items only for quality issues. Change of mind: minus the $3.00 return fee. Over $50: team lead approval.

## 24. Hygiene — entering the production floor
Wear a uniform, cap and mask, wash hands for 30 seconds, and pass through the air shower to enter the production floor. No rings, watches or phones.

## 25. Hygiene — freezer temperature
Keep the freezer at 0°F or below and log it every 2 hours. If it stays above 5°F for more than 30 minutes, call the quality lead immediately. If the quality lead doesn't answer, call the head of production.

## 26. Hygiene — food handler card
Employees who handle food renew their food handler card every year. If it expires, they may not enter the production floor until it is renewed.

## 27. Training — required annual training
Every employee completes harassment prevention, privacy, workplace safety and disability awareness training each year. Missing training lowers the year-end review.

## 28. Training — job training support
Job-related outside training is supported up to $1,000 per year. Apply with form HR-260 and submit the certificate within 2 weeks of finishing. If you don't finish a supported course, you pay the fee back.

## 29. Employee clubs
A club can register with 10 or more members and gets $10 per member per month. Register with the general affairs team on form GA-501.

## 30. Vehicles — company cars
Reserve a company car in groupware at least one day ahead. After driving, log the mileage and fuel receipts in the vehicle log. In an accident, notify the insurer and the general affairs team right away.

## 31. Vehicles — parking
Headquarters parking is provided only for executives, employees with disabilities, pregnant employees, and carpools of 3 or more. Apply with form GA-520.

## 32. Benefits — health checkups
Employees aged 40 or older get a full health checkup every year, under 40 every 2 years. The company covers up to $500 per person. Spouses are covered up to $300 every 2 years.

## 33. Benefits — child education support
High school tuition for employees' children is covered at actual cost. College students get up to $1,000 per semester, for up to 2 children.

## 34. Benefits — cafeteria
Lunch in the company cafeteria is free; dinner is free only for employees who signed up for overtime. Sign up for overtime in groupware by 5 PM.

## 35. [Revised 2026-07-01] Higher travel meal limit
To reflect rising prices, the travel meal limit goes from $10 to $12 per meal starting July 1, 2026. All other travel meal rules stay the same.

## 36. [Revised 2026-09-01] More remote work
Starting September 1, 2026, office staff may work remotely up to 2 days a week instead of 1. The approval deadline (6 PM the day before) and the exclusion of floor staff stay the same.
